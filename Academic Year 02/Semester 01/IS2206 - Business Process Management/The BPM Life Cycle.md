The **BPM Life Cycle** is a continuous, circular framework used to manage and improve business processes through six distinct phases. Each phase produces specific outputs that feed into the next, ensuring that organizational goals remain aligned with operational execution.
# Phase 01: Process Identification

- **Goal:** To systematically define the organization's set of business processes and establish criteria for prioritizing them.
- **Key Activities:**
    - Aligning BPM objectives with the organization's **strategies and goals**.
    - Identifying and enumerating all existing processes.
    - Prioritizing key processes based on **Importance** (strategic relevance), **Dysfunction** (health/trouble level), and **Feasibility** (susceptibility to BPM initiatives).
- **Result:** A **Process Architecture**, which is a conceptual framework (Level 1 to Level 3) showing the processes and their interrelationships.

# Phase 02: Process Discovery

- **Goal:** To gather information about an existing process and organize it into an **As-is process model**.
- **Techniques:** Information is gathered using evidence-based discovery, interviews with participants, or workshops.
- **Modeling:** This phase utilizes **BPMN** (Business Process Model and Notation) to create a visual representation of activities, events, and resources to improve efficiency.

# Phase 03: Process Analysis

- **Goal:** To identify the strengths and weaknesses of a process and understand their impact on organizational performance.
- **Two Types of Analysis:**
    - **Qualitative Analysis:** Focuses on identifying and eliminating **waste, redundancies, or losses**.
    - **Quantitative Analysis:** Uses **numbers, figures, and statistics** to focus on metrics like time, cost, and capacity.

# Phase 04: Process Redesign

- **Goal:** To create a **To-be process model** that addresses the weaknesses identified during analysis.
- **Two Approaches:**
    - **Continuous Process Improvement:** Seeking incremental solutions to issues one at a time.
    - **Business Process Re-engineering (BPR):** Reworking the entire process structure to make it significantly more effective.

# Phase 05: Process Implementation

- **Goal:** To transform the redesigned To-be model into an **executable process model** and put it into operation.
- **Execution Methods:**
    - **Systemic:** Implementation using specific software and **BPM Suites (BPMS)**.
    - **Non-Systemic:** Implementation without the use of dedicated BPM tools.

# Phase 06: Process Monitoring and Controlling

- **Goal:** To track and measure the implemented process on a continuous basis to ensure it meets performance goals.
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Common metrics include process **duration, cost, capacity, and error rates**.
- **Feedback Loop:** The data collected here determines if the process has become "bad" over time and requires a new cycle of improvement, starting again at the discovery or identification phase.
# Key Concept: As-is vs. To-be Models

- **As-is Process Model:** Represents the **current state** of the organization's processes, helping to visualize breakdowns and areas of risk.
- **To-be Process Model:** Represents the **future state**, allowing the organization to model the impact of changes before they are actually implemented.
