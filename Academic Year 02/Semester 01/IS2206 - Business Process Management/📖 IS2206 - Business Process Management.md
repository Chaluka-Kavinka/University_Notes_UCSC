#Lesson-Plan 


> [!NOTE] Goal
> The primary goal of this lesson is to provide you with a comprehensive understanding of the **organizational practices of Business Process Management (BPM)**. You will learn to recognize specific business processes and apply techniques to re-engineer them for better efficiency.

---
# Intended Learning Outcomes (Learning Objectives)

Upon completing these lessons, you should be able to:

- **Describe** the different phases of the BPM life cycle.
- **Model** "As-is" (current) and "To-be" (future) processes using Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN).
- **Assess** how Information Technology (IT) can support process management.
- **Identify** weaknesses in processes and apply improvement techniques or best practices to fix them.
- **Apply** change management theories to various business contexts.
- **Improve** your ability to communicate effectively in group settings.

# Topics Covered

1. [[Introduction to Business Processes]]: Definitions, elements (events, activities, actors), and types of processes.
2. [[BPM Life Cycle and BPM Suites]]: The phases of management and the IT infrastructure that supports them.
3. [[Process Identification]]: Defining a company’s process set and creating a process architecture.
4. [[Business Process Modeling (BPMN)]]: Using standard notation to map "As-is" and "To-be" states.
5. [[Process Discovery]]: Techniques for gathering information and ensuring model quality.
6. [[Process Analysis]]: Using qualitative and quantitative methods to find weaknesses.
7. [[Process Redesign]]: Rethinking processes to improve time, cost, and quality.
8. [[Process Implementation and Monitoring]]: Executing models and tracking KPIs.
9. [[Change Management]]: Handling organizational transitions.
